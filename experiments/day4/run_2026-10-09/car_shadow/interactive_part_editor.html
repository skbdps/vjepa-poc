Scope of this result. Sparse scores compare predicted parts with approximate assistant-authored polygons. The initial frame alone prompts tracking; there are no later corrections. Masks can include nearby pixels. SAM 2 may have seen DAVIS in training.

Each enabled part is edited after subtracting every other predicted mask. Overlap pixels and disabled parts stay unchanged. This protects predicted regions, not anatomical truth. This is deterministic recoloring, not generative video editing or a face-identity result.
